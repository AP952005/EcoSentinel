# EcoSentinel — 3-Minute Competition Demonstration Script

**Target Duration:** 2 minutes 45 seconds (allowing 15s timing buffer)

---

### **[0:00 – 0:25] Introduction & Welcome**
- **Visual:** EcoSentinel landing page with interactive mascot welcoming the viewer.
- **Narration:** *"Welcome to EcoSentinel, an environmental intelligence platform designed for the Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026. Environmental officers face thousands of clearance applications each year, struggling to verify historical footprints and continuous compliance against satellite data. Today, we'll demonstrate how EcoSentinel connects deterministic Earth observation with bounded GenAI investigation."*

### **[0:25 – 0:50] Global Intelligence & West Kalimantan Showcase**
- **Visual:** Navigate to `/global-intelligence`. Interactive round globe spins into Indonesian Borneo (West Kalimantan).
- **Narration:** *"We begin in Global Intelligence. We enter coordinates in Ketapang Regency, West Kalimantan. The system automatically calculates a 5 km geodesic buffer covering 7,800 hectares, loading 4 environmental catalogue modules: Sentinel-2 vegetation trends, Hansen historical forest loss, Dynamic World land-cover transitions, and disturbance context."*

### **[0:50 – 1:20] Multi-Temporal Analysis & 12-Point Scrubber**
- **Visual:** Scrubbing through the 12-point chronological observation timeline from 2021 to 2024. Side-by-side comparison displays baseline forest vs candidate clearing.
- **Narration:** *"EcoSentinel avoids synthetic gaps by retrieving real temporal checkpoints. Here, our 12-point timeline shows healthy canopy in 2021 with mean NDVI of 0.82. In late 2022, an 18.4-hectare vegetation decline drops NDVI to 0.44, corroborated independently by the 2022 Hansen tree cover loss layer."*

### **[1:20 – 1:55] ProofLoop™ Signature AI Interrogation**
- **Visual:** Clicking 'Continue Analysis (ProofLoop)'. The Gemini 3.8 Flash panel expands, highlighting supporting vs contradictory evidence.
- **Narration:** *"Instead of generic AI summaries, EcoSentinel launches ProofLoop powered by Gemini 3.8 Flash in low-thinking mode. ProofLoop interrogates the candidate finding. While spectral drops and Dynamic World confirm bare ground expansion, ProofLoop highlights that zero thermal hotspots were detected, weakening any claim of fire or illegal burning. Crucially, it identifies unverified cadastral lease boundaries and recommends the Next-Best Evidence: requesting official concession decrees and high-resolution drone verification."*

### **[1:55 – 2:25] Visual Evidence Report (PDF) & Watchtower**
- **Visual:** Downloading high-resolution PDF report and opening `/watchtower` to save continuous monitoring cadence.
- **Narration:** *"With a single click, officers generate a high-resolution First Environmental Report containing the results register, counter-evidence matrix, and legal disclaimers. We then convert this investigation directly into an automated Watchtower schedule, monitoring for future Sentinel-2 observations every 14 days."*

### **[2:25 – 2:45] Conclusion & Google Cloud Architecture**
- **Visual:** Mascot success pose and architecture status card highlighting Cloud Run, Vertex AI, and Earth Engine.
- **Narration:** *"By grounding Gemini 3.8 Flash in deterministic satellite measurements, EcoSentinel turns raw Earth observations into accountable, evidence-backed environmental protection. Built for Google Cloud Run and Google Earth Engine. Thank you."*
