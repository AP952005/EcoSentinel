# EcoSentinel — Pitch Deck & Slide Outline

### Slide 1: Title & Theme
- **Title:** EcoSentinel — Accountable Environmental Intelligence Platform
- **Sub-title:** Bounded AI Investigation & Multi-Temporal Earth Observation
- **Theme:** Sustainability & Social Impact (Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026)

### Slide 2: The Core Problem
- 10,000+ environmental clearance applications submitted annually across sensitive tropical biomes.
- Clearance officers are overwhelmed by hundreds of pages of unverified EIA filings.
- Critical lag between ground deforestation and regulatory intervention.

### Slide 3: The Solution — EcoSentinel
- Three connected workflows:
  1. **Global Intelligence:** Screen any coordinate on Earth across multi-temporal satellite records.
  2. **FER & Shadow Footprint:** Automated screening of approved Zone A vs surveillance Zone B.
  3. **Watchtower:** Continuous automated surveillance cadences.

### Slide 4: Signature AI Innovation — ProofLoop™
- Powered by **Gemini 3.8 Flash** in token-efficient low-thinking mode.
- Not a generic chatbot: executes bounded counter-evidence interrogation.
- Separates supporting evidence from sensor artifacts and phenological noise.
- Pinpoints the **Next-Best Evidence** required to establish ground truth.

### Slide 5: Real Earth Observation Datasets
- **Sentinel-2 Surface Reflectance (10m):** Multi-temporal chlorophyll and vegetation trends.
- **Hansen Global Forest Change (30m):** 20+ years of historical canopy loss records.
- **Dynamic World (10m):** Sub-pixel near real-time land-use transitions.

### Slide 6: System Architecture & Google Cloud Foundation
- **Compute:** Google Cloud Run (Containerized FastAPI backend & Next.js frontend).
- **AI Engine:** Gemini 3.8 Flash via Vertex AI / Google GenAI SDK.
- **Geospatial Engine:** Google Earth Engine.
- **Data & Storage:** Google Cloud SQL & Google Cloud Storage.

### Slide 7: Demonstration Case: Ketapang, West Kalimantan
- Demonstrates 18.4 ha canopy decline in Indonesian Borneo.
- 12-point chronological timeline tracking NDVI shift from 0.82 to 0.44.
- ProofLoop evaluates thermal absence and recommends cadastral decree verification.

### Slide 8: Future Roadmap & Impact
- Integration of high-resolution Planet NICFI and GEDI LiDAR canopy height models.
- Native mobile inspection portal with offline geospatial caching for forestry field officers.
